# All-Worker Congress Election Discord Announcement Design

## Purpose

Give players enough information to understand the All-Worker Congress election, see the current political landscape, and submit meaningful influence actions before the campaign closes on Friday, 26 June 2026.

## Format and tone

Use a hybrid bulletin: begin with a brief in-world election announcement, then switch to concise player-facing instructions. The post should feel politically alive without obscuring what players need to do.

## Required content

- State that the projections are provisional and may change before certification.
- Give the deadline as Friday, 26 June 2026, without inventing a specific closing time.
- Explain that players influence the election through tickets and favours.
- Ask each ticket to identify the acting character or party, favour source, target Trade Federation, favours spent, and a description of the action.
- Explain in plain language that ideologically well-matched efforts are stronger and repeated concentration on the same party/federation combination has mild diminishing returns.
- Mention that parties and players may coordinate broader campaigns and that GMs will adjudicate unusual or freeform actions.
- Do not publish numerical multipliers, percentage-point formulae, or other backend calculation details.
- Include the current provisional Congress result: ALLB 6, NCC 5, SRF 6, Education 2, LRA 8; 27 seats total; no Independent seats.
- Include the current projected Council result: ALLB 3, NCC 3, SRF 3, Education 1, LRA 5; 15 seats total.
- End with a compact, copyable ticket template.

## Success criteria

The finished post is ready to paste into Discord, makes the Friday deadline unmistakable, distinguishes Congress seats from Council seats, and gives players a clear next action without exposing GM-only mechanics.
